METHODOLOGY BLUEPRINT: THE CLOSED-SYSTEM ATTENTION CAPACITY ENGINE

1. THE CENSUS DENOMINATOR LOCK (THE ABSOLUTE TIME BUDGET CEILING)
THE ENTIRE ENGINE IS ANCHORED ON INDEPENDENT SOVEREIGN Census HeadcoUNTS FOR EACH 
OF THE EIGHT LAUNCH NATIONS (U.S. CENSUS BUREAU, ONS, INSEE, DESTATIS, INE, ISTAT, 
IBGE, INEGI). THE MODEL ISOLATES THE DEMOGRAPHIC AGE HEADCOUNT FOR THE P13+ 
POPULATION WITHIN EACH TERRITORY AND MULTIPLIES THAT HEADCOUNT BY A RIGID, 
NON-ELASTIC AWAKE TIME STANDARD OF EXACTLY 16 ACTIVE AWAKE HOURS PER DAY OVER A 
STANDARDIZED 30-DAY MONTHLY CYCLE. THIS ARITHMETIC REVEALS A IMMUTABLE TOTAL MARKET 
ATTENTION CAPACITY CEILING FOR EACH REGION. REGARDLESS OF HOW MANY INDEPENDENT APP 
DEVELOPERS OR TELEVISION Currency PANELS CLAIM MASSIVE AUDIENCE EXPANSIONS, THE 
COMBINED ATTENTION PROFILE HOURS GENERATED WITHIN OUR CLOSED MARKET SPONGE CAN 
NEVER EXCEED OR DRIFT PAST THIS HARD CENSUS POPULATION CEILING.

2. METRIC NORMALIZATION MATRIX (CONVERTING VANITY METRICS TO FOCUS HOURS)
THE MODEL ELIMINATES THE FRAGMENTED SILO LANGUAGES DESIGNED TO PROTECT INFLATED 
LEGACY VALUATIONS (REACH, UNIQUE VISITORS, GRPS, FREQUENCY) AND CONVERTS ALL PUBLIC 
DATASETS INTO A SINGLE CURRENCY: MILLIONS OF ABSOLUTE ATTENTION HOURS PER MONTH.
• TRADITIONAL GLASS INGESTION: LOCAL BROADCAST CURRENCY DISCLOSURES PROVIDING DAILY 
  TIME SPENT VIEWING (TSV) ARE MULTIPLIED BY THE CENSUS COHORT HEADCOUNT WEIGHTS 
  AND PROJECTED OVER 30 DAYS TO ISOLATE CRUDE LINEAR FOCUS VOLUMES.
• DIGITAL PIPELINE INGESTION: GLOBAL WALLED-GARDEN METRICS REVEALED IN WALL STREET 
  INVESTOR RELATIONS EARNINGS DISCLOSURES (DAILY ACTIVE USERS / MONTHLY ACTIVE USERS) 
  ARE PRO-RATA APPORTIONED VIA LOCAL REGIONAL MARKET PENETRATION DATA AND MULTIPLIED 
  BY INDEPENDENTLY DISCLOSED APPLICATION TRACKING PARAMS TO ISOLATE DIGITIZED FOCUS 
  HOURS.

3. THE ZERO-SUM SQUEEZE AND DIARY DE-DUPLICATION ENGINE
BECAUSE MULTI-TASKING AND CONCURRENT SCREENING RUN RAMPANT (E.G., SCROLLING TIKTOK 
WHILE A TELEVISION BROADCST PLAYS IN THE BACKGROUND), A NAIVE ADDITION OF DIGITAL 
AND GLASS METRICS WOULD CAUSE THE DATA TO SPILL PAST THE IMMUTABLE CENSUS MAXIMUM. 
THE ENGINE APPLIES LOCALIZED SLIDING-SCALE COHORT DUPLICATION COEFFICIENTS DERIVED 
DIRECTLY FROM RECURRING PUBLIC DIGITAL TRACKING SURVEYS AND CONSUMER PANEL DIARIES. 
DIGITAL HANDSET USAGE—WHICH DEMANDS PHYSICAL ENGAGEMENT VIA EYE AND THUMB FOCUS 
TO EXIST—IS TREATED AS HARD PRIMARY ATTENTION CAPITAL. THE TRADITIONAL TELEVISION 
GLASS HOURS ARE PROGRAMMATICALLY SQUEEZED DOWN AND COMPRESSED BY THE CORRESPONDING 
DUPLICATION MULTIPLIER UNTIL ALL CONCURRENT OVERLAP IS FLATTENED, DE-DUPLICATED, 
AND FORCED TO FIT PERFECTLY INSIDE THE HARD CEILING OF THE FINITE DAILY CLOCK.

4. THE NESTED FUNNEL SAFETY GUARD
TO SAFEGUARD THE ARCHITECTURE AGAINST DATA-INFLATION OUTLIERS, ALL REGIONAL TRACKING 
ARRAYS ENFORCE RIGID HORIZONTAL AND VERTICAL INTEGRITY BOUNDARIES. INTERNAL STATE 
LOGIC PROGRAMMATICALLY PROHIBITS NARROWER, YONGER DEMOGRAPHIC GENERATIONAL TIERS 
(13-44 YOUTH, 13-34 CORE, 13-24 GEN Z) FROM EVER REVEALING OR OUTPUTTING A HIGHER 
ATTENTION VOLUME TIER THAN THE BROADER DEMOGRAPHIC CELL LAYERS (13-54 WORKFORCE, 
ALL CONSUMERS) THAT ENCAPSULATE THEM. EVERY CALCULATION CURVE DECAYS SMOOTHLY FROM 
LEFT TO RIGHT, HONORING LOCALIZED POLICY BUFFER COEFFICIENTS.
